# City Air Tracker

Compares air quality across U.S. cities and Divisions over time, using OpenWeather's modelled air pollution data from November 2020 onward.

> **What this file is for:** our shared vocabulary. Use these exact words in code, Jira tickets, PRs and on the dashboard; avoid the words under _Avoid_. If a new concept appears or a word starts meaning two things, add or fix it here in a PR. It is a glossary only: decisions go in [team-decisions.md](team-documentation/team-decisions.md), design in [docs/architecture](docs/architecture/README.md).

## Places

**Tracked City**:
One of the U.S. cities the product follows, identified by fixed coordinates and belonging to exactly one Division.
_Avoid_: location, place, coordinates

**Division**:
One of the nine U.S. Census divisions; the unit used to compare parts of the country.
_Avoid_: region, area, zone

## Data

**Raw Response**:
One OpenWeather history response kept unchanged, together with the request that produced it and when it was retrieved.
_Avoid_: raw data, raw record, payload

**Daily Reading**:
The air quality of one Tracked City on one date: the worst hourly AQI Level of that day plus the mean concentration of each pollutant. A pollutant with no data stays empty.
_Avoid_: observation, record, row, measurement

**AQI Level**:
OpenWeather's 1–5 air quality index with its label (Good, Fair, Moderate, Poor, Very Poor). Not the U.S. EPA AQI.
_Avoid_: EPA AQI, air score

**Division Figure**:
A Division's air quality for a period, weighted by each Tracked City's metro population: mean pollutant concentrations and the share of residents' days at each AQI Level. Never a plain average across cities.
_Avoid_: regional average, division mean

## Guidance

**Health Tip**:
Fixed, EPA-based guidance text chosen by rule from a Daily Reading's AQI Level. Never generated by AI.
_Avoid_: advice, recommendation, AI tip

**Insight**:
A plain-language explanation of how a Tracked City's air quality behaved over a period, produced with AI help.
_Avoid_: summary, enrichment, AI explanation

## Operations

**Pipeline Run**:
One execution of the stages, recorded with its trigger, status, and counts.
_Avoid_: job, execution

**Backfill Run**:
A Pipeline Run that loads a historical date range, such as the full history since November 2020.
_Avoid_: initial load, full load

**Incremental Run**:
A Pipeline Run that adds only the dates not yet loaded, normally yesterday.
_Avoid_: daily job, delta load
